Gaming Montage Maker

Automatically download multiple YouTube videos, extract custom-duration clips from each, and combine them with your choice of audio!

Automated Workflow:
  • Enter multiple YouTube URLs (one per line)
  • The app will automatically download each video
  • Extract random segments from each video (excluding first/last 30s)
  • Combine all segments into one final video
  • No manual input required after providing URLs!
